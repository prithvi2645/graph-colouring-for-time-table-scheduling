🧠 Algorithm Walkthrough

Step-by-step trace of the Greedy Graph Coloring algorithm assigning each subject to a time slot.

📐 Algorithm

Greedy Graph Coloring — processes each vertex and assigns the smallest color not used by any of its neighbors.

⏱️ Complexity

Time: O(V² + E)
Space: O(V + E)
V = subjects, E = conflicts

✅ Guarantee

No two conflicting subjects share a time slot. Zero scheduling conflicts guaranteed.
